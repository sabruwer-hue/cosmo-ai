PERSONAL AI WORKSPACE

Cosmo Core — your personal intelligence workspace.

Cosmo Core is the personal AI package within the Cosmo platform. It is being built to help users turn questions, ideas and ongoing projects into structured conversations, plans and knowledge records.

What it is designed to do

  • Have a focused AI conversation and preserve useful history.
  • Organise projects, missions, decisions and notes.
  • Search saved knowledge and connect related records.
  • Separate evidence, inference and speculation instead of presenting every idea as fact.
  • Support careful planning, research and follow-through.

Honest development status

Cosmo Core has a live backend foundation and a chat gateway, but external web research and some advanced memory capabilities are still being developed and verified. Do not treat every planned feature as already available.

Package access

The package licence is designed as a once-off lifetime purchase linked to your account. Optional cloud, AI or third-party services may require separate memberships or usage credits. Prices will be confirmed before checkout is enabled.
