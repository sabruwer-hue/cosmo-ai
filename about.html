ABOUT COSMO

Practical intelligence, built around real work.

Cosmo AI is a South African modular software project bringing together personal AI assistance, work organisation and QR-powered retail information. The idea is simple: technology should help people think clearly, organise work and make useful information easier to access.

One account, purpose-built packages

Cosmo is being developed as a family of separate packages. Cosmo Core focuses on personal intelligence and knowledge. Cosmo Work focuses on tasks, projects and workflows. Cosmo QR Retail focuses on product information, QR shelf labels and stock-related workflows for retailers.

Clear ownership and fair access

Package licences are designed as once-off lifetime purchases tied to a customer account, not to one installation or device. Optional tools may have separate memberships or usage charges when they rely on ongoing cloud services, AI processing or third-party integrations. Prices and limits must be shown before purchase.

Built carefully

Cosmo is being released in stages. Some foundations and prototype workflows exist while other capabilities remain in development. We aim to label what is available accurately, protect customer data and verify payment before granting paid access.
