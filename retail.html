How the shelf hub works

1. Create the product

Enter product details, shelf price, photo and stock information.

2. Generate a QR label

Print and place the QR sticker next to the product on the shelf.

3. Customer scans

The public page displays the latest product information, live specials, low-stock status, next delivery and nearest recorded expiry date.

4. Keep it updated

Record stock movements, batches, expiry dates and incoming supplier orders. The QR link remains the same as product information changes.
