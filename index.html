TRANSPARENCY FIRST

What is available today?

Are all Cosmo tools already live?

No. Core authentication, database foundations, chat gateway, task workflows, owner administration and the first QR Retail data services have been built. Some modules are still under development and will be marked clearly as they are verified.

Can customers subscribe and pay today?

Not yet. The product and tool catalogue and billing data structure are being prepared. Payment provider onboarding, checkout, verified webhooks and subscription entitlement enforcement must pass testing before sales begin.

Will every customer see my private Cosmo history?

No. Customer records must remain isolated by account. The private creator administration portal is separate from customer package access.

Will the QR shelf labels stay current?

They use a stable QR link to a product information hub. The page reads the latest saved product price, active promotion, stock, recorded expiry and incoming delivery information. Accuracy depends on store records being kept up to date.
