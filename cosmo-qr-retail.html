QR PRODUCT INFORMATION FOR RETAILERS

Cosmo QR Retail — make every shelf scan useful.

Cosmo QR Retail is designed for shops that want a simple QR code on a product shelf or label. A shopper scans with an ordinary phone camera and opens a product information page without needing to install an app or create an account.

Customer-facing product information

  • Product name, image and description.
  • Current price and unit price where relevant.
  • Active promotions and product information.
  • Availability status, shown only from recorded store stock.
  • Expiry, batch and safety information where the retailer has supplied and verified those records.

Retailer tools

  • Create QR codes for products and variants.
  • Design printable shelf labels with product image, QR code and price.
  • Maintain product records and stock movements.
  • Plan low-stock alerts, replenishment and supplier workflows.
  • Support multiple branches and staff roles as those modules are released.

Accuracy and privacy

Public product pages should reveal only customer-safe product information. Internal supplier details, purchase orders and exact stock records remain private. Stock and restock information is only as accurate as the retailer's records.

Availability

Cosmo QR Retail is in staged development. The QR product hub and stock-management specification is in place, but the full retailer experience and payment flow must be verified before it is marketed as generally available.
