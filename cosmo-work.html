Cosmo Work — keep work moving.

Cosmo Work is the operational workbench in the Cosmo product family. It is designed to help individuals and small teams turn projects into clear tasks, ordered steps and accountable decisions.

Work management features

  • Create and organise projects and tasks.
  • Break work into steps with visible progress.
  • Track items waiting for approval.
  • Keep operational records linked to the work they belong to.
  • Use a separate work portal for day-to-day execution.

Development status

The current platform includes task, step and approval foundations. Team collaboration, scheduled automation and integrations are separate capabilities and should only be considered available when verified in the live product.

Access model

The Cosmo Work package is intended to use a once-off lifetime licence. Optional hosted services and capacity features may be offered separately with clear pricing.
